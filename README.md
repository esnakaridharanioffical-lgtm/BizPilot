# BizPilot

## AI-Powered Business Intelligence Platform

BizPilot is an AI-powered business intelligence platform designed to help small businesses understand their data, monitor performance, identify trends, predict future outcomes, and receive actionable business recommendations.

## Problem Statement

Small businesses often have valuable sales, inventory, expense, and customer data but may not have the tools or technical expertise to analyze it effectively.

BizPilot aims to convert raw business data into simple, meaningful insights that can support better decision-making.

## Core Features

- Business data upload
- Sales and revenue analytics
- Expense and profit analysis
- Interactive business dashboard
- Trend analysis
- Sales/demand prediction
- Business alerts
- AI-powered recommendations

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Streamlit
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Plotly
- Scikit-learn
- SQLite
- Git & GitHub
